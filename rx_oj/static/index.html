LOWER IS BETTER
01 / BASELINE

For each testcase, use the fewest cycles from correct Rust builds at optimization levels 0, 1, 2, 3, s, and z.

02 / SCORE

Divide your cycles by the baseline, then take the geometric mean across all optimization testcases. A score of 1.0 matches the baseline.

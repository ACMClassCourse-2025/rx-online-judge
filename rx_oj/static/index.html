    How scoring works

    Your score is the geometric mean of your cycles divided by the Rust baseline for each optimization testcase. Cycles are summed across every input of a testcase. The baseline uses the fewest correct cycles from Rust optimization levels 0, 1, 2, 3, s, and z.

    All four suites must pass. Your best accepted optimization submission against the current catalog counts; equal scores share a rank. Expand a score to see its testcase ratios.
